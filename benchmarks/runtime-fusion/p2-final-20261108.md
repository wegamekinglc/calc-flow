## Frozen scope and contract

The maintained `scripts.benchmark_suite.decay_warm` controller uses
`paired-warm-stream-v1`: one fresh baseline/candidate worker pair per case,
persistent native jobs with history loaded before appends, and 30 alternating
AB/BA append pairs. Four workloads produce 120 pairs and 240 timed appends.
These are advancing-state warm samples, not the release gate's two-round
fresh-process experiment or a standalone minimum comparison.

All cases use Float64 Rows, default `stable_v1`, normal GC, four Tokio workers,
one entity, and exactly 1,000 history rows in a 1,000-row segment. Thus W=1,000
is full before timing. Affinity remains the same inherited guest CPUs 0–31 on
both arms. The first three cases are targets; the fourth measures the remaining
single-row COW cost. No Cartesian matrix or extra workload was run.

The unchanged 20,000-resample bootstrap estimates the median of paired
baseline/candidate ratios. The quotient of the displayed individual P50s is
not that estimator. The tool reports `accepted=true`, target indexes `[0,1,2]`
and `confirmed_regressions=[]`. No case has upper CI below `1 / 1.05`.
Every saved statistic was reproduced exactly from raw samples.

The timer covers prepared Data enqueue through source/task/channel handling,
rolling, watermark finalization, projection, sink delivery and Arrow conversion.
COW preparation, certification, reseeding and fallback remain inside timing.
Startup, planning, history, validation, IPC and shutdown are outside each sample
and inside the phase budget. Checkpoint throughput and restore are not measured.

Timing variation remains visible in the raw distributions. In case 3 the
candidate CV is 0.702 and P95 is 2.837 ms, versus P50 1.028 ms; its paired
median interval still exceeds the declared speedup gate. This local warm
experiment establishes no cross-machine or tail-latency guarantee.
